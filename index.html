01

Strategy note

The one line
I build public figures.
I help established experts turn expertise into intellectual capital, then build influence around it.
The idea

Fame as we know it is dead. The public figure is rising.

You do not need to be famous to be successful. You need to be recognized. Expertise alone is no longer enough; your ideas have to travel.

A public figure is not a celebrity, but a recognized authority whose ideas travel farther than the job title.

How the brands speak
Torund Always present. She is the face and the origin of the idea.
The Library The podcast and everything published. The brand people meet most often.
The Agency Client facing work: advisory, architecture, execution.
The Press Only when an idea is ready to become a book.
The order they speak in
Origin
Origin thinks
Torund originates the thinking.
Asks: What do we think?
Signal
Signal communicates
The Library puts the thinking into public circulation.
Asks: What do we want the world to think about?
Forge
Forge builds
The Agency and Press turn that thinking into architecture, assets and influence.
Asks: What are we going to build?
Thinking  →  conversation  →  creation. Nobody should have to learn four brands to understand the work.
02

Who we speak to

The audience

Primarily Gen X and Millennials who were raised for a world that no longer exists. They were taught there was a path: get educated, build expertise, do good work, let institutions establish credibility. That world has changed.

Increasingly, whether we intended to or not, we are all becoming public figures.
The client

Established experts at a crossroads: CEOs, executives, founders, professors, advisors, business owners. The expertise already exists. Something has changed, and what got them here is no longer enough to get them where they want to go.
